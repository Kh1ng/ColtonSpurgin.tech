# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary users are potential employers and clients evaluating Colton Spurgin's work. They need to understand his capabilities, inspect credible examples, and find a clear way to contact him.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Colton's professional profile and selected work. It succeeds when a qualified visitor can quickly judge his fit for a role or project and starts a conversation.

## Positioning

Colton combines software development, infrastructure and cybersecurity experience, applied AI study, and academic research. His work includes complete systems rather than isolated demos.

## Operating Context

Visitors typically arrive from a job application, referral, GitHub profile, or LinkedIn profile. They scan the site before deciding whether to examine a project or contact Colton.

Colton is a senior at the University of West Florida. He specializes in artificial intelligence and expects to graduate in December 2026. He is also a lead software developer intern and a university research assistant.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site is a React and Vite application hosted on GitHub Pages.
- The main paths are the introduction, project index, and Traveling Salesman Problem report.
- Colton's lead internship work demonstrates substantial software development and technical leadership. Any detailed public case study requires his approval.
- Colton's university research applies AI in an area adjacent to computational biology.
- Public descriptions of the research must remain high-level until Colton approves specific details.
- Future project additions should not require a redesign of the site structure.

## Brand Commitments

Use the name Colton Spurgin and the `coltonspurgin.tech` domain. The voice should be direct, technically credible, and free of inflated claims.

## Evidence on Hand

- Current portfolio copy and project data in `src/pages`.
- Project screenshots and report graphics in `src/assets`.
- Source repositories linked from the project cards.
- GitHub and LinkedIn profiles linked from the home page.
- Separate internship and research workspaces were reviewed during setup. They are private working evidence, not approved public sources.
- No testimonials, client outcomes, or employment metrics are currently available. Future work must not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with evidence that helps an employer or client judge Colton's work.
2. Explain technical depth in language a hiring manager or client can scan.
3. Support claims with projects, artifacts, or source code.
4. Keep restricted or unfinished research details private until approved.
5. Make new work easy to add without restructuring the portfolio.
